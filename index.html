Justice, awareness, and empowerment

Founded in 2021, Amar Online School-AOS is a non-profit organization dedicated to protecting and promoting children’s rights across Bangladesh. The work is built on justice, awareness, and empowerment, so that more children can grow up informed about their rights and able to use them.

Every child deserves a safe and informed childhood. Amar Online School-AOS helps children realize their rights, raise their voices, and shape a better future.

What we work toward

  1. Promote and safeguard children’s rights.
  2. Educate communities about child marriage and child labor.
  3. Raise awareness of climate change and environmental responsibility.
  4. Engage children in campaigns, signature drives, and pledges.
  5. Bring child-related issues into the media.
  6. Run quizzes, Olympiads, and essay contests.
  7. Share learning and awareness content with young people.

Key initiatives

Human Rights Quiz & Essay

With United for Human Rights, Dhaka Chapter, 587 students across Bangladesh joined a quiz and essay competition on fundamental rights. Forty winners were recognized.

General Knowledge Olympiad

More than 700 students took part, building curiosity, critical thinking, and knowledge across subjects.

Daily UNCRC sharing

The UN Convention on the Rights of the Child, shared article by article so young people can learn their rights online.

Awareness campaigns

Programs on climate, environmental responsibility, and child protection, including a daily campaign through the 30 articles of the Universal Declaration of Human Rights.

Media advocacy

Coverage in Bengali news has carried the organization’s work further, putting children’s issues in public view.

UNICEF recognition

In the 18th UNICEF Meena Media Awards 2023, the founder’s submission was named one of the top five in Text – Below 18, from more than 1,000 entries.

Founder

Prottush Islam

Founder and Executive Director

Prottush Islam founded Amar Online School-AOS in 2021. He began writing on social issues as a Child Journalist with UNICEF Bangladesh. His articles cover child marriage, women’s rights, juvenile crime, street children, and gaming addiction.

  • Child Journalist, UNICEF Bangladesh
  • Nominee, UNICEF Meena Media Awards 2023 — top five, Text – Below 18
  • Nominee, International Children’s Peace Prize, 2022

The nomination

On 2 April 2024, UNICEF Bangladesh wrote to confirm the nomination and invited him to the award ceremony at the Pan Pacific Sonargaon Hotel, Dhaka, on 22 April 2024.

The independent panel reviewed more than 1,000 entries. Sheldon Yett, Representative, UNICEF Bangladesh, signed the notification.
