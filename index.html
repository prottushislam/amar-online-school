Our mission

The realisation of children’s rights across Bangladesh.

Amar Online School-AOS is an independent non-profit founded in 2021. The aim is a safer, more informed childhood, especially for children from marginalized communities.

What we do

AOS advances children’s rights in Bangladesh through learning, public campaigns, and media.

We stand with children

The daily work is rights education: UNCRC articles, the Universal Declaration of Human Rights, and plain-language conversations about marriage, labor, and the climate.

We work toward

  1. 01Promote and safeguard children’s rights.
  2. 02Educate communities about child marriage and child labor.
  3. 03Raise awareness of climate change and environmental responsibility.
  4. 04Engage children in campaigns, signature drives, and pledges.
  5. 05Bring child-related issues into the media.
  6. 06Run quizzes, Olympiads, and essay contests.
  7. 07Share learning and awareness content with young people.

Key initiatives

Competitions, daily lessons, and campaigns that put children’s issues in public view.

Human Rights Quiz & Essay

With United for Human Rights, Dhaka Chapter, 587 students across Bangladesh joined a quiz and essay competition on fundamental rights. Forty winners were recognized.

General Knowledge Olympiad

More than 700 students took part, building curiosity, critical thinking, and knowledge across subjects.

Daily UNCRC sharing

The UN Convention on the Rights of the Child, shared article by article so young people can learn their rights online.

Awareness campaigns

Programs on climate, environmental responsibility, and child protection, including a daily campaign through the 30 articles of the Universal Declaration of Human Rights.

Media advocacy

Coverage in Bengali news has carried the organization’s work further, putting children’s issues in public view.

UNICEF recognition

In the 18th UNICEF Meena Media Awards 2023, the founder’s submission was named one of the top five in Text – Below 18, from more than 1,000 entries.

Founder

Prottush Islam

Founder and Executive Director

Prottush Islam founded Amar Online School-AOS in 2021. He began writing on social issues as a Child Journalist with UNICEF Bangladesh. His articles cover child marriage, women’s rights, juvenile crime, street children, and gaming addiction.

  • Child Journalist, UNICEF Bangladesh
  • Nominee, UNICEF Meena Media Awards 2023 — top five, Text – Below 18
  • Nominee, International Children’s Peace Prize, 2022

On 2 April 2024, UNICEF Bangladesh confirmed the Meena Media Awards nomination and invited him to the ceremony at the Pan Pacific Sonargaon Hotel, Dhaka, on 22 April 2024. An independent panel reviewed more than 1,000 entries. Sheldon Yett, Representative, UNICEF Bangladesh, signed the notification.
